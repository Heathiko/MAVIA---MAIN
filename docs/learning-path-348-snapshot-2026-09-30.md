11. Choosing the Right Technique
   - [Lesson_Separating-Mixtures | lo881 | text] Choosing the Right Technique
     Some mixtures need more than one technique used in order. To recover both the salt and the sand from a mixture of salt, sand, and water, a learner would first filter the mixture to trap the sand, then evaporate the filtered liquid to recover the salt. Planning the correct sequence is the most demanding part of this competency.
