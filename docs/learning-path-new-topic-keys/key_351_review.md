## Required links (19)

- organs_overview -> brain: the overview defines what an organ is and places the brain on the body map, which the brain section then builds on.
- organs_overview -> heart: a learner needs the idea of an organ as a body part with a special job, and where the chest organs sit, before studying the heart's job.
- organs_overview -> lungs: the overview's definition of an organ and its body map come before the details of what the lungs do.
- organs_overview -> stomach: the stomach section assumes the learner already knows what an organ is and that the stomach is a major organ in the belly.
- organs_overview -> liver: the liver section assumes the general idea of an organ and its place among the major organs on the body map.
- organs_overview -> kidneys: the kidneys section assumes the learner knows what an organ is and that the kidneys are major organs near the back.
- organs_overview -> bones_muscles: the overview lists bones and muscles as major organs, which frames the section on what they do.
- stomach -> intestines: the small intestine breaks food down "even more", so the learner must first know that the stomach mashes food into a paste.

- heart -> protective_bones_figure: the rib cage figure shows the heart inside the ribs, so the learner must already know what the heart is and where it sits.
- lungs -> protective_bones_figure: the rib cage figure shows the lungs inside the ribs, so the learner must already know what the lungs are and where they are.
