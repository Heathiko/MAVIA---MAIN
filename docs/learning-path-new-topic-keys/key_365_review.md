## Required links (13)

- environment -> people_depend: saying people get their needs "from the environment" needs the learner to know what an environment is.
- basic_needs -> people_depend: the section walks through air, water, food and shelter one by one, so the learner must first know these are the basic needs.
- environment -> animals_depend: the carabao, bird, fish and frog examples show animals using mud, trees, ponds and water, the parts of an environment.
- basic_needs -> animals_depend: each animal example shows a basic need (food, shelter, air) being met.
- environment -> plants_depend: plants taking sunlight, water, air and soil from their surroundings only makes sense once the learner knows what an environment holds.
- basic_needs -> plants_depend: the section explains how plants meet their needs, including sunlight to make food, which is introduced with the basic needs.
- animals_depend -> aquarium_model: the aquarium asks what the fish and snail get from the tank, applying how animals get food, air and shelter.
- plants_depend -> aquarium_model: the aquarium asks what the kangkong gets from the tank, applying how plants use water, light and air to make food.
- animals_depend -> depend_on_each_other: animals eating plants or other animals, and using plants for shelter, builds on how animals get food and shelter.
- plants_depend -> depend_on_each_other: the food chain starts with a rice plant using sunlight to make its food.
- people_depend -> environment_changes: dirty rivers and smoky air hurting people (lost fish, hard to breathe) needs knowing that people get food and air from the environment.
- animals_depend -> environment_changes: cutting trees taking away birds' homes needs knowing that animals get shelter from the environment.
- plants_depend -> environment_changes: garbage killing water plants and rain washing away soil needs knowing that plants need clean water and soil.
